Crenshinibon - The Crystal Shard

Overview

Crenshinibon is a magical relic created by seven liches who had set out to create the most vile and evil artifact ever.

Abilities

Crenshinibon can convert sunlight into magical power. If it is left lying in the sun it can absorb the light to unleash into powerful magical effects later.

This means that it is also reliant on sunlight. If the stored power has been used up and it isn't in contact with direct sunlight then it cannot be used to create or sustain any magical effects.

Crenshinibon can be used to create magical towers. The outside of the towers looks exactly like an upscaled version of the crystal shard iteslf. The inside contains rooms, doors, and staircases, and can be used as a wizard's tower.

Crenshinibon seems to be indestructible. Powerful divine beings have tried to smite it, and it has been thrown across the Planes of Existence, and it has not been damaged at all.

Personality

Like many magical artifacts Crenshinibon has a personality, a will, and desires. It cannot speak, but it can project its desires onto its wielder, making them have the same desires. The wielder is generally not aware that they are being influenced by the crystal shard.

The most obvious giveaway that someone is under the control of Crenshinibon is that they want to raise as many towers in the likeness of Crenshinibon as they can get away with.

Aside from raising the towers, Crenshinibon also causes its wielder to seek conquest.

Evil creatures in the vicinity of Crenshinibon are magically drawn in and tempted to wield the crystal shard. If Crenshinibon already has a wielder, then any newly attracted evil creatures are compelled to follow the command of the wielder.
