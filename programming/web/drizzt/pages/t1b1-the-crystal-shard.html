The Crystal Shard

The Icewind Dale Trilogy - Book 1

Prologue

The book starts on the demon Errtu in the Abyss. He is awaiting word on the magical relic Crenshinibon. He is enraged and kills the messenger when he is informed that Crenshinibon has been thrown across the Planes of Existence.

Unbeknownst to Errtu, but very beknownst to us, the Crystal Shard lands on the Prime Material Plane far in the northern mountains of Icewind Dale.

Some time after that a group of wizards from the Hosttower of the Arcane in Luskan arrives in Icewind Dale.

They mainly pretend to be there to buy scrimshaw.

But there is another reason why they came to a remote location where people regularly go to disappear. Some of the wizards manage to talk Akar Kessel, a wizard apprentice, into murdering his master, Morkai the Red.

Meanwhile on the shore of Maer Duraldon

Regis the halfling is napping by the lake, too lazy to actually do anything.
