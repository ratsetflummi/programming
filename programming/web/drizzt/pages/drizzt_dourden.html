Drizzt Do'Urden

Drizzt Do'Urden is a drow elf and the main character of The Legend of Drizzt.

Originally he was meant to be a side character supporting Wulfgar's adventures in the Icewind Dale Trilogy, but fan feedback about Drizzt was so overwhelmingly positive that the story shifted to focus more on Drizzt and his ties to the drow.
