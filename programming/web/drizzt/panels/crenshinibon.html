Crenshinibon - The Crystal Shard

see also: the main page about Crenshinibon.

Crenshinibon – also known as The Crystal Shard – is a magical relic created by seven liches who had set out to create the most vile and evil artifact ever.

It attracts evil creatures and temps them into wielding it.

Once it has a wielder it manipulates their mind, making them think that Crenshinibon's desires are their desires.

It mainly uses this magical control to push its wielder to pursue conquest and to magically raise towers that are an exact likeness of Crenshinibon. (No one really knows why it wants that.)
