Regis is a halfling. He is lazy and cowardly, caring only about his next meal, his next nap, and to stay out of trouble.

He is kind of bad at the staying out of trouble part.

The reason why he is in Icewind Dale is because he is in hiding.

He is originally from Calimport, a place far south, marked by heat and deserts. Regis was part of a thieves' guild back home, until he stole a Magical Ruby from the guild master.
Regis has been on the run from an assassin sent by the guild master ever since.
